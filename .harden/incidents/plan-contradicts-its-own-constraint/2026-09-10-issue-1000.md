# Incident: plan-contradicts-its-own-constraint (file 2)

**Batch:** `.harden/findings/issue-1000.jsonl`, rows 2 and 4 (both `fixed`
in `e798d3f3`).

## Symptom

Two review findings against a change that implemented its published plan
(https://github.com/rotnov/pycc/issues/1000#issuecomment-5624198134)
faithfully. The plan listed a "new accepted file" case among the rule tests
that call `check_file` on strings, while its own diff section made status
`A` unconstrained one tier earlier, so the test could never reach the tier it
was assigned to (row 2). The plan defined the index-only stub by marker
*membership* ("base contains the exact line") and then unfroze the stub body
by *position* (indices 6-10), so detection and action modelled the shape
differently (row 4). Four adversarial review rounds on the plan verified
plan-vs-tree and plan-vs-issue, not plan-vs-itself.

## Root cause

Same mechanism as the seed: a constraint stated in one section and a work
item in another are never read together during the plan's review loop. Gap
type: content (plan). Batch count: 2. Prior topic files: 1 (the seed,
issue #80, three members). Cumulative: 5 findings across 2 plans.

## Termination point

`.claude/skills/issue-to-plan/SKILL.md`, step 7 — the review-round contract.

## Artefact (proposed, not shipped)

Rung: `rule` inside the plan gate (review-tier to plan-tier promotion; no
static check can decide that two passages disagree). Proposed insertion after
the bullet `an explicit "considered, no change, because X".`:

> Every round also runs a self-consistency pass: for each constraint the
> draft states (a rule, the tier a case is handled at, an index or field a
> check keys on), the reviewer names the work item and the test that satisfy
> it and confirms no other work item or test contradicts it. A test the plan
> assigns to a tier the plan's own architecture makes unreachable, or a
> predicate that detects a shape differently from how its action consumes
> it, is a plan defect, not an implementer's.

Not shipped from this task: a `rule` artefact needs an arena verdict before
it lands (harden step 6), and the skill edit is outside issue #1000's scope.
Two topic files keep a textual artefact eligible; a third occurrence
disqualifies it and the next rung is a review-check.
