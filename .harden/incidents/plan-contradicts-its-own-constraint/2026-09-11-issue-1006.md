## What happened

Row 5: the published plan for #1006 stated in its record specification that
`attestation.milestone_line` is verbatim at the *subject* commit, then in
its validator item prescribed comparing it with `docs/ROADMAP.md` at `HEAD`,
and in its re-collection item allowed re-collecting only against
`origin/main`. Read together, a pull request that changes the roadmap's
current milestone could never carry a green record: the validator demands
the new line, the collector can only produce the old one. The dispatched
implementer built the `HEAD` comparison as written; `ievo:deep-reviewer`
round 4 caught the deadlock. An independent advisor round resolved the fork
toward the subject-scoped side: `verify_git` proves the line via
`git show <subject>:docs/ROADMAP.md`, `validate()` keeps only a shape check,
D-241 records the bounded window a transition pull request opens.

Row 6: the same plan pinned `scripts/test_check_status_snapshot.py` on the
collector's fixture as "the collector's suite" while its work item 4 named
`scripts/test_collect_status_snapshot.py` as the collector's suite; the
implementer copied the pairing into `docs/WEBSITE.md` and D-241.
