# Recurrence: a dated freshness stamp drifts from the content it certifies

**Date:** 2026-09-04
**Topic:** own-change-falsifies-adjacent-prose
**Verdict:** build nothing — counter only (recurrence 7)
**Source:** `/harden batch .harden/findings/issue-923.jsonl`, findings 1 and 2

## Symptom

Two members of one class in the #923 deep review, failing in opposite directions:

- `docs/ROADMAP.md:7` — `Last reviewed on 2026-08-26.` was not bumped although the
  commit's hunks land at lines 30-40, 135-217 and add 237-238, all inside or under
  the `## Current delivery status` section that line introduces. A *stale stamp over
  changed content*. Fixed in-diff.
- `site/status/index.html:62` — `dateModified` bumped to 2026-09-04 while lines 158
  and 405-415 still assert the known-false "v0.4 not started". A *bumped stamp
  certifying prose that is false against tracker state*. Deferred to umbrella #802.

## Gap type

**Trigger.** A guard for the ROADMAP line exists — `.claude/skills/next-milestone/SKILL.md`
step 6 couples the stamp to the `Current milestone:` line, and its own parenthetical
records this same line drifting silently once before. #923 was an `issue-implement`
run, so the procedure holding the guard was not executing. Wrong fork, not disobedience.

## Promotion identified, and why it is not built

The class splits on feasibility, and only one half is even decidable.

*ROADMAP half — decidable.* Predicate: if `base..head` changes any line of
`docs/ROADMAP.md` inside the `## Current delivery status` section other than the stamp
line itself, the stamp's date must differ between base and head. Section bounds are
recomputed per revision from the heading regex; the file holds exactly one
`^Last reviewed on (\d{4}-\d{2}-\d{2})\.$` line inside that section, and the tree's two
other freshness stamps use different forms and sit outside it, so the anchor cannot
collide. `scripts/check_status_page_freshness.rb` is the working precedent for the
shape — two-revision `git show`, two-dot `git diff --name-only`, bounded retry for
shallow CI checkouts, fail-closed on an empty diff — and most of the ~120 lines of Ruby
would be its plumbing verbatim, in its own workflow because `ci.yml` is a D-103
protected target. False positives are near zero.

**Rejected on gate quality, not on undecidability.** The gate is satisfied by a
one-token date bump. It converts "last reviewed on" into "last touched on" and destroys
the semantic the stamp carries: an agent that edits one table cell learns to bump the
date, and the stamp stops meaning anyone reviewed the section. This is the same
reasoning `documentation-sweep-stops-at-the-changed-file/2026-09-03-issue-795.md` used
to reject the roadmap-hunk gate — a gate agents learn to satisfy with filler is worse
than no gate. A stale stamp has never caused a wrong merge decision, and this one was
caught at `note` severity and fixed in-diff for free.

*Site half — not decidable.* `site/status/index.html`'s `dateModified` is already pinned
per slug in `scripts/check-site.sh` PAGE_SPECS (enforced at `:1739-1740`, sitemap
`lastmod` cross-checked at `:2177`), so the bump was a deliberate, gate-approved
two-file edit. What is false is English prose about live tracker state, which no static
checker can evaluate. The one decidable sliver — the contradiction between
`docs/ROADMAP.md:12` "v0.4 in progress" and the site's "v0.4 not started", both already
in `required_visible_text` — is exactly the #802 pinned-literal deadlock this finding
was deferred into; a second pinning gate would tighten that deadlock, not relieve it.

A textual pointer in `issue-implement` was weighed and declined: the adjacent topic
`documentation-sweep-stops-at-the-changed-file` already holds 8 files and two entries
that planted and then had to content-edit an `issue-implement` step-5 sentence, which is
the ladder's own evidence that another sentence there is the weaker artefact.

**No change to the standing verdict.** The review tier caught both members at
note/warning severity at zero production cost and both were disposed of correctly
in-diff. The counter is the artefact.
