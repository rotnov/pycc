# Incident: a citation is attached to a scope wider than what it covers

**Batch:** `.harden/findings/issue-918.jsonl`, class C. Found by the pinned
`ievo:deep-reviewer` — the only finding it returned.

## Symptom

`docs/ROADMAP.md:32` listed four annotation positions that still reject
parameterized containers and attached `(#925, #926)` after the list. Two issues
track two of the four positions; the sentence reads as if each of the four were
tracked. It contradicts `docs/ROADMAP.md:151`, `docs/PYTHON_STANDARDS.md:326`,
and the conformance-breadth manifest, all of which scope the citation correctly.

Rescoped in `95e51835`.

## Root cause

**Gap type: content.** A trailing parenthetical citation has no syntax for
scope: `(#925, #926)` after a list of four binds to whatever the reader assumes.
The sentence was rewritten during a rebase onto #928's condensed roadmap text,
and the condensation merged two separately-scoped clauses into one list without
re-deriving which citation covered which member.

around the edit was not re-derived" — but the specific failure here is narrower:
the edit *was* re-derived, and the citation's scope silently widened as a side
effect of merging two lists.
