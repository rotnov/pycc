**Date:** 2026-09-02

**Verdict:** build nothing — the existing discipline fired and was correct
**Batch:** `.harden/findings/issue-869.jsonl`, finding 2 (naming-behaviour), round 1

## Symptom

One `note`-severity finding on `crates/pycc_artifact_layout/src/lib.rs`
questioned whether the `CARGO_TARGET_DIR` clause in the missing-`pycc_rt`
diagnostic is a non-actionable pointer on the arm where the target triple
was never built anywhere. The reviewer marked it non-actionable itself. On
verification the clause was kept on both arms deliberately: the plan's
rejected-alternatives section had already considered host-arm-only wording,
the cross-target arm shares the redirect failure mode, and pycc cannot
suppress the clause conditionally without the config discovery D-216 closed
as permanent. Disposition: refuted, no change.

## Root cause

Not a failure, and the same one the three prior entries record. The pinned
reviewer generates hypotheses with read-only tools and no view of the plan's
recorded alternatives; a share of its output is necessarily a re-derivation
of a tradeoff the plan already settled. Verifying every finding against the
plan and the governing decision records before acting is what turns that
into signal.

The nuance this occurrence adds: the reviewer flagged its own finding as
non-actionable. A self-qualified note still enters the pile — it is data
about where the reviewer's context ends (here: the plan's
rejected-alternatives section), not a defect to fix.

## Termination point

None. Escalating would build a guard against a defence that worked.
Counter recorded so the topic stays visible if the *verification* step is
ever the thing that fails — across four occurrences it has not.

## Host journal

and the lesson (verify before acting) is already policy. No entry.
