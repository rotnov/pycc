## What landed

`MirExpr::TupleLiteral` now passes each element scalar through the existing
`retain_if_int_duplicate` at ingress, so a tuple field holds a reference of its
own instead of aliasing whatever supplied it. Overwriting the supplying name can

## The accepted leak, and its successor

The ingress retain has no matching release, so a supplier rebound inside a loop
leaks one object per trip. Measured with `/usr/bin/time -l` on the authoring
host: peak RSS is flat at ~1.9 MB before the change (each iteration's object was
freed — precisely the use-after-free), against 26 116 096 B at 500 000 trips and
50 200 576 B at 1 000 000 trips after it, i.e. 1.92x for a 2x trip count.

Closing it is [#636](https://github.com/rotnov/pycc/issues/636) — "release a
`Ty::Tuple` slot's owned `int` fields at slot death, and take a reference on
whole-tuple copy" — filed in milestone v0.3 and blocked on D-124's container
refcounting. Both halves are required together: a slot-death release without a
copy retain fires twice against one ingress reference, which is a double-free.
