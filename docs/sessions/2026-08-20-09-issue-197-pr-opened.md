## The part worth reading before touching this area again

Three rounds of the pinned local reviewer. Rounds 1 and 2 are fixed on the branch. Round 3's two
blockers are real but pre-existing, and the work of proving that is the reusable part.

Round 2 found two mutation tests passing for the wrong reason: they exited non-zero because an
earlier block had left a shared fixture dirty, not because of the mutation under test. Round 3
found the class again. Rather than deleting guards one at a time — O(n) under a suite that stops
at the first failure — every guarded validator invocation was instrumented with a line marker and
its stderr captured, then paired with the block's own "validator accepted X" string. Mismatches
are the vacuous sites: **18 of them**, not 2.

Running the same instrumentation at `1fdf3511` and diffing the two message sequences is what
separated "fix here" from "file separately". Exactly one site changed, and it was verified
non-regressive by deleting the new guard and watching the site fall back to its previous
rejection. The 18 are filed as https://github.com/rotnov/pycc/issues/644 together with the
`README_PATH` shadowing at `check-site.sh:2046` as contributing root cause.

One caution for whoever picks up #644: inserting a marker line inside a backslash-continued `if`
breaks the continuation and silently drops the environment override, which presents as a state
fragility that does not exist. Skip insertion when the previous line ends in a backslash.
