## What landed

[PR #626](https://github.com/rotnov/pycc/pull/626) squash-merged as
`21b012f7`, closing [#624](https://github.com/rotnov/pycc/issues/624)
(Part 1 of #146). It adds `rc: Cell<u32>` refcounting to `BigIntObj` with
`BigIntObj::new` as the sole constructor, the
`pycc_rt_bigint_retain`/`_release` entry points, and guarded retain/release
emission at named-storage and loop-induction sites, with an inline
`(w & 3) == 0 && w != 0` test in front of every call so ordinary smallint
code never reaches the runtime symbol. D-180 records the narrowing of
D-058's blanket "never freed" concession and enumerates the residual set
that remains.

Three problems surfaced after the first commit and are all resolved on
main:

1. A codegen-depth IR test was missing entirely — the guard chain could
   have regressed to an unguarded call on an inline fixnum with every
   black-box test still passing. Added in-crate, since
   `compile_to_object_with_observer` is private.
2. That new test crashed `native-build-test (windows-latest)` with
   `STATUS_ACCESS_VIOLATION`: its helper called inkwell's `module.verify()`
   directly, reaching past this crate's `verify_module` wrapper, which is a
   no-op under `#[cfg(windows)]` for D-029's reason. Fixed by deleting the
   call — `compile_to_object_with_observer` already runs `verify_module` on
   that exact module before invoking the observer.
3. The static guard built to stop (2) from recurring shipped with a comment
   claiming the crate owns "exactly two safe entry points" when D-029
   records three. Caught by the post-harden review round and fixed: the
   scan now tripwires the third protection (the `ManuallyDrop` at
   `TargetTriple` creation, whose wrapping spans several lines and cannot
   be proven by a line-oriented scan) and states per-protection depth
   instead of implying uniform coverage. The same round removed a
   false-positive: the printer check had been keyed on the literal receiver
   name `module`.

`/harden batch` clustered the round's findings into two classes and is
recorded under `.harden/incidents/`:
`platform-wrapper-bypassed-by-new-code` (shipped artefact, `verify: manual`
with violator output for A–E) and
`coverage-claimed-without-executing-the-check` (`verdict: pending`,
deliberately no artefact — the review tier is the right rung and it fired
on both instances). The process lesson is in `docs/AGENT_RETROSPECTIVE.md`.

Two defects found in passing were filed rather than folded in:
